Device Checks

Two test pages. Open each one, press its button, and read the list.

Set Auto-Lock to Never before you start. Settings, Display & Brightness, Auto-Lock. The playback page takes a few minutes, and a phone that locks its screen pauses every video on the page, which turns a good run into a page of red. The page tries to hold the screen awake by itself and will warn you if it cannot.

Video playback Seven questions about how this phone plays video. A few minutes. Leave it alone while it runs. Right-to-left mirroring Six questions about English and Arabic. Answers itself straight away.

Reading the results

Green Works and red No are findings. Amber Not tested is not: it means a clip did not arrive in time, or the thing being checked never happened. Five of the seven playback rows can say it.

Each page has a Copy the results button when it finishes. Copy and send them rather than typing them out — the detail lines matter more than the verdicts.

Worth doing twice

Once normally, and once with Low Power Mode switched on. That is the one thing no desktop browser can answer, and it is the case where iOS refuses to start video at all.
